## Integrated pre-soak gate

Before a provider-backed run, demonstrate the exact assembled release on fresh
synthetic/recorded inputs through the public operator interfaces:

1. Acquire/retain attributed inputs and revisions; create or update the correct
   case with duplicate, delayed and missing-event behavior.
2. Produce a finalized structured decision or abstention with its required
   evaluation evidence. Unknown research completion remains visible. Prove the
   autonomous scheduling/dispatch path; a sequence driven only by a test harness
   does not establish autonomous operation.
3. Apply server-owned admission and allocation, then the declared paper execution
   model and conserved ledger. Exercise competing proposals, rejected/deferred
   work and partial/unknown effects, as well as a successful paper lifecycle.
4. Read back attribution and operator actions; run a model/feed outage and pending
   obligation restart/restore without losing protection or duplicating economics.
5. Retain failed attempts and source/image/schema/runtime receipts. Keep this
   synthetic rehearsal database separate from prospective observations.

The present service's executable strategy remains a synthetic `DEMO` fixture.
A real-input soak needs an explicit compatible executable baseline and instruments,
its declared admission/evidence status, and verified routing from those inputs.
A healthy collector or active research cases cannot substitute for that path.

## Run declaration and report

Prepare the declaration before enabling provider/model use or applying an exact
release. Use existing authorization where it covers that concrete scope; request
only missing access, budget or activation authority after preflight is reviewable.
Current exhausted diagnostic budgets do not become a recurring model budget. Record bounded request/token/time/cost limits, provider and account
environment, access readiness, retry limits, source coverage and stop behavior;
keep keys out of chat and Git. Live orders and Augr production/data are outside
this plan. Existing pilots, Switchyard services and the curtailed observer remain
untouched; the completed overnight schedule remains disabled.

Select a natural window that includes the intended market sessions. Record the
actual UTC and monotonic start/end, expected feed cadence, maximum observation
and worker gaps, minimum usable evidence and failure/stop conditions. Do not force
entries to reach an activity target. Insufficient natural activity remains an
insufficient sample and may require a separately declared extension.

Retain counts and identities for inputs/revisions/cases, decisions/abstentions,
proposals/defer/reject reasons, reservations/orders/fills/cancellations,
positions/cash/fees/slippage, unknown effects and reconciliations. Measure feed
coverage, deadline and worker progress, error/recovery history, ledger conservation,
operator interventions and baseline-relative outcomes. Preserve exact source,
image, schema, configuration, dataset and strategy/evaluation/model versions.

Report functional reliability separately from strategy performance and statistical
qualification. A passing operational soak can expose losses or insufficient
samples; it does not establish profitability or authorize Alpaca real-money use.
Do not erase failed windows or replace elapsed wall time with virtual tests.
